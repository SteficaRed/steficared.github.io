About Me

I completed my bachelor studies at the Faculty of Mathematics, University of Belgrade, in the program Statistics, Actuarial and Financial Mathematics. During my career, I worked as a mathematics and informatics teacher in primary and secondary schools, and later as a Survey Analyst at the Statistical Office of the Republic of Serbia, in the sampling methodology group.

In November 2025, I enrolled in master studies at the Faculty of Organizational Sciences, University of Belgrade, in the Information Engineering program. I completed all exams with the highest grade, 10.0. My academic focus is machine learning, big data, mathematical foundations of data science and software development for data science.

My master thesis focuses on the robustness of deep learning models for 6D object grasp pose estimation using degraded RGB-D data and point clouds.
